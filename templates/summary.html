Project Presentation & Viva Quick Reference

Q1: What is the core problem solved? Colleges face classroom underutilization (e.g. 50 students in 120-seat halls) alongside sudden overcrowding. This project optimizes room allocations based on predicted demand.
Q2: Why not use deep learning? For tabular attendance and timetable records with 5,000 rows, classical regression (Linear Regression & Random Forest) provides higher interpretability, fast training, low latency, and zero overfitting risk.
Q3: How does the recommendation algorithm work? A multi-constraint scoring engine: +40 for capacity sufficiency, +30 for operational availability, +15 for equipment, +10 for department preference, +5 for proximity, and a bonus for minimum unused capacity.
