How Data Cleaning Works

Pandas Steps
  • Step 1: df.drop_duplicates() eliminates duplicate timetables or attendance rows.
  • Step 2: pd.to_datetime() standardizes date formats to YYYY-MM-DD.
  • Step 3: Median imputation fillna(df[col].median()) fills missing numeric attendance or capacities safely without skew.
  • Step 4: Categorical mode imputation fills missing subjects or departments.
  • Step 5: Capacity and attendance bounds validation (clipping negative or impossible numbers).
